GoldenPaw

Stories of the world's fastest guardian

Not entirely of this place. Not entirely human. Across mountains, ruins, and the quiet places where old things wait, GoldenPaw answers the calls that no one else can hear.

The Stories

Asia

  1. Story One

    The Second Frequency

    Jiankou, the Great Wall — Huairou, China

    A signal that matches no known equipment pulses from the wild, broken heart of the Great Wall. An old man has been waiting eleven years for someone to give a notebook to. And a stone that calls once a decade is entering its final night.

  2. Story Two

    The Sound in the Stone

    Thyangoche, the Khumbu — Nepal

    A village burns at 5,400 metres, and men in black thermal suits are climbing away from it carrying detonators. Below the moraine, a shaft descends to something that has been humming since before the monastery was built. And a nine-year-old boy is the only one who knows what was in the box.

  3. Story Three

    Into the Wind

    Above Thorong La, the Annapurna massif — Nepal

    A storm window is closing over the Annapurna Circuit, and a twelve-year-old boy who followed a fox is somewhere above the cairns. The rescue is the easy part. On the long way back through the western passes, the wind carries something GoldenPaw set aside three years ago — and it has been tracking him ever since.

North America

  1. Story Four

    The Impact Layer

    Upheaval Dome, Canyonlands — Utah, United States

    A sulfur smell drifts from a crater that geologists have argued about for half a billion years, and someone with no permit knew exactly where to drill. The cylinders they're loading hold something sealed in the rock for sixty million years. And a man walking a memorized route into the dark carries the first real answer to what happened to GoldenPaw in the mine.
